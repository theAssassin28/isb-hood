ISB Hood MVP: Requirements Document
Group 12, SWPM Term 5 · Draft v0.2 · Hyderabad campus only · Companion to the Solution Hypothesis, MVP and Prioritization deck · Items marked [confirm] need a team decision or a fact we have not checked
1. Purpose
ISB Hood passes what one ISB batch learns about courses and careers to the next. This MVP is an experiment, not a small version of the product. It exists to answer one question:
Shape: one core loop (write a structured entry, read it, ask the author), run as two tests in sequence. Every part of the loop is needed: without any one of them we cannot tell whether the loop works.Test 1, Courses (primary): alumni and seniors write course reviews, and Co'27 students change a Term 7 bid because of one.Test 2, Career (one week later): people who made a career move write it down, and readers act on it. It runs now, not next term, because placement season is live, the same contributors are being contacted anyway, and it reuses Test 1's form, page and comments (about one extra day of build). It starts only once Test 1 has 50% of its supply target, so course results stay readable on their own.Pass lines and decisions: section 9.

4. User stories and acceptance criteria
Reader
Story
Acceptance criteria
As a student bidding for electives, I want to see what a course is really like so I can bid with fewer surprises
Course page shows: review count; median weekly workload; evaluation mix; quant-to-qual score; grade-risk split; learning value with agreement level; share who said the outline matched; top 3 "who should take it" and "who should skip it" lines; professor and term on every review
As a reader, I want to know if reviews still apply
If the current professor differs from the reviewed one, or the course has no reviews, the page shows a "New or changed course" banner
As a career pivoter, I want to read entries from people with my background
Career list can be filtered by target track and pre-MBA background; a "Like me" toggle applies the reader's own profile background
As a reader with a follow-up doubt, I want to ask the author
Any signed-in user can post a comment under an entry; the author gets an email within 5 minutes; replies are public and threaded
As a reader, I want to filter courses
Course list filters: term offered, workload band, quant or qual, track it helps; sort by review count or learning value
Contributor
Story
Acceptance criteria
As an alum, I want to review a course in about 5 minutes
Form opens from a link with the course preselected; under 15 fields; progress saved; median completion time logged
As a contributor, I want to control how I appear
Choose "Show my name" or "Verified Co'26 student". Moderators always see the real name
As an author, I want to answer questions without being flooded
Email alert per new comment, with a daily digest option; one-click link to reply
As an author, I want to fix or remove what I wrote
Edit and delete available on own entries at any time

6. Screens
Sign in: readers use Google sign-in limited to the ISB email domain [confirm domain for PGP YL]. Contributors arriving from an outreach link can use any Google account; their entries wait for moderator verification and then show a "Verified CoXX" badge. First visit asks programme (PGP or PGP YL), cohort, pre-MBA background, target track and how they heard about it.
Home: two entry points, Courses and Careers; search bar; "Write a review" button; empty state explains what the site is and asks first contributors to start.
Course list: cards with course name, current professor, review count, workload band, quant-or-qual tag, "New or changed" badge.
Course page: summary block (section 4), then individual reviews with professor and term, then comments under each review.
Career list: filters for track, company, background and the "Like me" toggle; playbook and company-note cards.
Entry page: full playbook or company note with author line and comment thread.
Write: choose course review, playbook or company note; form per section 5; preview before publish.
My activity: my entries, questions waiting for my reply, edit and delete.
Moderation: flagged items queue for moderators.
Every screen needs an empty state and an error state. A stranger should be able to finish "read a course page and ask a question" without help (test with 5 people, think-aloud, before launch).

8. Content rules and moderation
Review the course, not the person. No personal remarks about faculty, staff or students.
Any user can flag an entry or comment. Moderators act within 24 hours; flagged content stays visible unless it breaks the rules above.
Agree these rules with AAC/ASA before launch [confirm owner and meeting]. This is approval of content rules only; the MVP needs no ISB data or system access.
Anonymous authors show as "Verified Co'26 student" (or the right cohort); moderators can see who wrote it.

Outcome measures
Test 1: Courses
Test 2: Career
Supply pass
40+ reviews from 20+ contributors; 10+ Term 7 courses with 3+ reviews, by 3 Jan 2027
10+ entries: 5+ playbooks across 3+ tracks and 5+ company notes
Survey
Once, 11 to 17 Jan 2027 (after Term 7 bidding closes), to Co'27 readers who viewed a Term 7 course page. "Did ISB Hood change any of your bids?" Answers kept separate: changed (name the course), confirmed, no effect, did not use. Plus "How many seniors did you ask this time?"
Two weeks after first visit, to career readers: "Name one thing you did because of an entry." Coded as applied or skipped a firm, changed prep plan, chose an offer, contacted the author, or none
Minimum sample
30 responses
20 responses
Pass
20%+ answer "changed" and name the course. "Confirmed" is reported but not counted
30%+ name a concrete action
Both
70%+ of comment questions answered within 48 hours. Below the minimum sample the result is inconclusive: extend the survey, do not decide. We cannot observe real bids or shortlists, so these are specific, checkable self-reports, not observed behaviour
